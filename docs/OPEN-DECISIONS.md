# Open decisions: Riverside Family Eye Care rebuild

Decisions the practice (or the operator) can still change. The content pipeline (`src/build.mjs`, the port of
2026-10-01) applies a **default** for each one, so every page builds today; each default is reversible, says where it
is applied, and is also listed in `docs/BUILD-NOTES.md` section 4. "R" is the reference rebuild of another practice on
the same EyeCarePro platform (named in `docs/PORT-NOTES.md`). Nothing below is a fact the build invented: every number
comes from a file or a command named beside it.

## A. Decisions taken by the operator, as applied

1. **Robots come from the raw pages.** Every `<meta name="robots">` of each raw `<head>` is merged, most restrictive
   wins (`src/lib/seo.mjs sourceRobots / mergeRobots`). Result: the 23 pages noindex at source stay noindex (17
   archives, 6 `/template/*`); nothing else is noindex except `dist/404.html`. `audit/seo-inventory.json` (one robots
   meta per page) is not read for robots. Check: `node tools/seo-parity.mjs` (robots 148/148 equal to the raw merge).
   - Still open (Q7): `/404-page-not-found/` is `index` at source and stays indexable here. Recommendation: noindex
     (it is the platform 404 body). Change: one line in `page-model.mjs` (robots) or a site-map artefact rule.
2. **Unrendered shortcodes are resolved.** `[account get='name']` (on `/`, `/sitemap/`, `/whats-new/`) prints
   "Riverside Family Eye Care" and `[location get='city']` (on `/whats-new/`) prints "Fort Myers", both from
   `facts/client-facts.json` / `chrome.json`. Declared in `audit/clone-removals.json` `replacements` with the reason
   "unrendered platform shortcode, resolved to the account value it requests". Revert: remove `SHORTCODES` in
   `src/build.mjs`.
3. **The 3 `ecp-video` modules are kept as video embeds - with one deviation.** The decision said "keep them as
   YouTube embeds". The source modules are not YouTube: they are self-hosted `<video controls>` files
   (`ecp-video-type-media_manager`, `<source src="/wp-content/uploads/...mp4">`), and no YouTube URL for them exists
   anywhere in the 148 raw pages (the only YouTube link on the site is the separate eye-emergency video, which stays a
   YouTube iframe). Inventing a YouTube URL is not possible, so the build keeps the videos the way the source shows
   them: `div.embed.embed--video > video[controls][preload=none] > source`, pointing at the harvested files copied to
   `dist/media/` (3 files, 63,238,862 bytes; `audit/media-inventory.json`). Their poster images were never harvested,
   so the videos render without a poster (declared). **To decide:** ship 63 MB of video, or upload the three clips to
   the practice's YouTube channel (`@RiversideFamilyEyecare`) and switch the blocks to YouTube embeds.
4. **`/cherry-payment-plan/`: the widget did NOT work standalone, so the page takes the fallback - and needs one
   input.** The page's h1 is its own menu label, "Cherry Payment Plan" (the source page has no h1; its `<title>`,
   "Eye Care Services | ...", is kept as the title). The decision: embed the widget exactly as the source loads it "if
   it works standalone", else a clear link to the Cherry application URL found in the source markup. Evidence
   (`node tools/render-check.mjs --base <served dist> --paths /cherry-payment-plan/ --cherry`, headless Chrome, the
   exact source embed served from 127.0.0.1 with `RFEC_CHERRY=embed`; saved in `tmp/render-cherry.json` and
   `tmp/final-render-cherry.json`):
   - the widget script loads and draws only its static hero ("Treat now, pay later ..."); the calculator,
     how-it-works and FAQ mount points stay EMPTY; its API calls to `gql.withcherry.com` fail (`net::ERR_FAILED`: a
     cross-origin refusal of a non-practice origin), its analytics calls fail, and it throws one uncaught `TypeError`
     (an IntersectionObserver on a missing element);
   - it also loads **Segment analytics (`cdn.segment.com`)**: Cherry's tracker, not the practice's;
   - the source markup holds **no Cherry application URL** (the only Cherry URL in `audit/raw` is the widget script
     `files.withcherry.com/widgets/widget.js`; the widget's own buttons are scripts, not links).
   **Default applied (fail closed): link mode.** The page shows its label "Cherry Payment Plan" as a link to
   `RFEC_CHERRY_URL` when the practice supplies its Cherry application URL; until then the label renders with
   `data-needs="cherry application url"` (no URL is invented). No third-party script loads. **To decide:** (a) supply
   the application URL (`RFEC_CHERRY_URL=https://...`), or (b) test the embed on the practice's own domain (or a
   preview domain Cherry allows) and, if it works there, build with `RFEC_CHERRY=embed` (then the remaining
   cross-origin hosts on that one page are `files.withcherry.com`, `gql.withcherry.com`, `cdn.segment.com`,
   `fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`). The **site-wide floating "Pay over time" estimator is not reproduced**
   (a third-party script on every page); every page instead carries a local link to `/cherry-payment-plan/` labelled
   with that page's own menu label (the scaffold footer; `model.chrome.financing`). Both declared in
   `audit/clone-removals.json`.
5. **reCAPTCHA is never visible; forms are inert.** Both Gravity Forms are rebuilt field for field
   (`src/lib/forms.mjs`), including "Reason for Appointment" as a `<select>` with its 5 options and "Date of Birth" with
   its `mm/dd/yyyy` placeholder and screen-reader hint. The invisible reCAPTCHA, the honeypot and the Akismet block are
   dropped (declared). Each `<form>` carries `data-needs-backend="form endpoint"`, no `action`, no `mailto:`.
   - Still open (Q8): no "not connected yet" notice is authored (`chrome.json` `notice` is null). Since QA round 1
     (CONTENT-1) a submit without a backend sends nothing and keeps everything typed (`method="dialog"` plus a site.js
     cancel, with or without JavaScript); before, it posted every field to the page itself and the page reloaded empty.
     With no notice, a visitor who submits a valid form sees nothing happen. Decide the endpoint (and its spam
     protection) and whether a notice sentence (with 239-500-2020) should show until then; the dormant hook prints it as
     soon as `chrome.json` `notice` holds it.
6. **`/our-eye-doctors/` renders all 3 doctor cards with their bios** (Dr. Brittany Degler, O.D.; Dr. Kristin Nelson,
   O.D., IACMM; Dr. Maivys Longa, O.D.): photo or placeholder, name, the bio text the source prints (the platform
   truncates two of them with "..."), "Read More". Every team module on every page is lifted the same way (22 cards).
7. **Contact-lens brand pages print each product once.** The FULL product text (the hidden `#ecp-contactlens-full-N`)
   prints once; the platform's truncated excerpt (52 of 53 products; one cut inside an entity, "&nbs ...") is dropped
   and declared page by page in `audit/clone-removals.json` `sentences`; the script-only "Read More+" toggles are
   unwrapped to their words (`<p class="product__toggle">Read More+</p>`, no link). **To decide:** keep those 53 now
   functionless "Read More+" words, or declare them removed too.
8. **Links to the 16 live aliases stay links.** Each of the 25 in-content references to an alias is re-pointed,
   page-relative, to the page the live site redirects it to (`src/lib/seo.mjs movedFromAliases`, from
   `audit/site-inventory.json`). `dist/_redirects` and `dist/.htaccess` carry exactly those 16 source redirects and no
   other. Still open (Q4): 8 of the aliases land on one unrelated blog post ("Riverside Family Eye Care's Guide to Visual
   Hygiene on Electronic Devices"), so 12 links (for example "Dry eye syndrome (DES)" in `/winter-dry-eyes-2023/`) now go
   there, exactly as on the live site. Re-pointing them to the topical page (SITE-ARCHITECTURE section 10 lists the
   candidates) would be a behaviour change: decide per link.
9. **Content licence** - section C below.
